The five levels

From foundational needs at the bottom to self-actualization at the top.

  1. 1. PhysiologicalBasic physical needs, such as food, sleep, and bodily functioning.
  2. 2. SafetySecurity, stability, and protection from harm.
  3. 3. Love and belongingConnection, close relationships, and feeling included.
  4. 4. EsteemConfidence, competence, achievement, recognition, and respect.
  5. 5. Self-actualizationDeveloping abilities and realizing personal potential.
